What SPHERE does

Operational depth without the noise

Core surfaces biometrics teams use every day — designed for clarity under audit pressure.

1

Study home that stays with every deliverable

Protocol metadata, library status, and module entry points share one operational picture — so programmers, leads, and reviewers align without status meetings.

  • Sponsor, cutoff, subjects, and delivery targets
  • Raw · SDTM · ADaM · Programs · TLF · Logs
  • Direct paths into Mock Shells, Tracker, and Files
2

Program & TLF tracker

Group by analysis area. Filter by type, status, tags, and SAP section. Drive check-out, QC, and freeze from one board.

3

Row-level accountability

See who has a program checked out, shell lock state, job status, and the next action — Send to QC, Approve, or Freeze.

4

Mock shells that match the CSR

Catalog by number and type, edit titles and analysis sets, generate Word/RTF with footnotes and programming notes.

5

Status you can act on

Shell lock, job completion, and file editability sit beside the action that moves the row forward.

How it works

From study setup to package delivery

Four clear steps — the same rhythm your team already knows.

  1. 1

    Stand up the study

    Capture protocol, SAP, sponsor, cutoff, and library baselines on Study home.

  2. 2

    Author shells & programs

    Build mock shells, import programs, and bind them to SAP sections and tags.

  3. 3

    Track QC & freeze

    Move work through In dev → QC → Approved → Frozen with clear ownership.

  4. 4

    Deliver the package

    Assemble CSR or DMC outputs with files, define, and publisher in the same study context.
